# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the repository's hosting platform
(for example GitHub's private reporting features). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer
may remove content, or restrict participation, when that is needed to keep the project welcoming.
